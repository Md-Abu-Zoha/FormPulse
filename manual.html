What is FormPulse?

FormPulse is a browser extension that fills long, repetitive forms — job applications, registrations, portals — in seconds using a three-layer Hybrid Engine.

⚡

Layer 1 — Local Profile

Basic fields (name, email, phone, DOB) are filled instantly from your saved profile without any AI or internet call.

🧠

Layer 2 — Memory Vault

FormPulse remembers every unique answer you've typed into past forms and reuses them the next time a similar question appears.

✨

Layer 3 — AI Engine

Complex open-ended questions (cover letters, "Why this company?") are handled by a powerful LLM that reads your bio and writing style.

ℹ️
FormPulse uses these three layers in order — Local first, then Vault, then AI. This means most fields on a typical form never need to make an API call at all, making FormPulse fast, private, and cost-effective.

First-Time Setup

Get FormPulse ready in under 3 minutes. You only need to do this once.

1

Choose an AI Provider

Open the extension popup and go to Advanced Settings → AI Provider. Choose from Google Gemini (free, fast), Groq, DeepSeek, or Kimi. We recommend Gemini for new users as it has a generous free tier.

2

Enter Your API Key

Visit your chosen provider's website, create a free API key, and paste it into the API Key field. Your key is securely encrypted directly on your device and is never transmitted to any server other than the AI provider you chose.

✅
Groq Free Key: console.groq.com  |  Gemini Free Key: aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
3

Fill In Your Local Memory Profile

Under Advanced Settings → Local Memory, enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, City, LinkedIn, GitHub, Date of Birth, and any ID document numbers. These fields are used for instant local fills — no AI is ever called for these.

4

Write Your Professional Bio

Open the Professional Bio section. Write a detailed paragraph about yourself — your college, degree, skills, tools, years of experience, and the type of work you do. The better this bio, the better every cover letter and essay FormPulse writes for you.

💡
Be specific. Mention real skills ("MERN stack, n8n automation, Tailwind CSS"), not vague things ("I am a hardworking person").
5

Add Writing Samples (for Tone Matching)

Open the Writing Examples section. Paste 2–3 excerpts from emails or short paragraphs you have previously written. FormPulse's AI analyzes these and mirrors your exact vocabulary, tone, and sentence structure when writing cover letters — so it sounds like you, not a robot.

6

Save Your Settings

Click the Save Memory button at the bottom of the popup. A green confirmation toast will appear. You are now ready to auto-fill any form.

Auto-Filling a Form

How to use FormPulse on any form — job board, HR portal, registration page, or chatbot.

1

Navigate to the form page

Go to any website with a form you want to fill. FormPulse works on standard HTML forms, React-powered forms, custom modals, and most multi-step application wizards.

2

Click the FormPulse icon

Click the FormPulse extension icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the popup. Check that your profile is saved (you should see your name reflected in the fields).

3

Press "Auto-Fill Page"

Click the Auto-Fill Page button. FormPulse will scan the page, identify all fillable fields, and send them through the Hybrid Engine. Within a few seconds, the entire form will light up with colored borders showing what was filled and how.

4

Review the highlighted fields

Look at the borders on each field. Green = verified. Purple = AI-generated, usually great. Yellow = needs your attention. Dashed Grey = was already filled before you clicked.

5

Submit!

After a quick review, submit the form. FormPulse will automatically record any new question-answer pairs into your Memory Vault so future forms fill even faster.

AutoPilot Mode

Let FormPulse watch the page in the background and fill forms the moment they appear — no button clicks needed.

🟢

When AutoPilot is ON

FormPulse continuously watches the page for changes. The moment a new field appears (e.g., you click "Apply Now" and a dialog pops up), it immediately fills the new fields. No button click needed.

🔴

When AutoPilot is OFF

FormPulse does nothing passively. You are fully in control. Only when you explicitly click "Auto-Fill Page" does the extension interact with the form. This is the default mode.

⚠️
Important: AutoPilot will NEVER overwrite a field that already has content in it. If you are mid-typing in a field, AutoPilot will respect that and leave it alone. Only the manual "Auto-Fill Page" button can overwrite pre-filled fields (and only with verified Green data).

Best practices for AutoPilot

Long multi-page application forms (like Workday or Greenhouse portals) reveal new fields as you click "Next". AutoPilot detects each new page of fields and fills them instantly, so you only have to click "Next" and review, never type.
Some job portals use a conversational chat interface instead of a traditional form. FormPulse detects chat boxes and text inputs inside these chatbots and fills them. AutoPilot mode is especially useful here since new question inputs appear one at a time.
If the AI skips a field (returns empty with a Yellow marker), AutoPilot will not repeatedly retry that same field and spam the API. It marks the field as "sentToAI" and moves on, so you're never charged double for the same question.

Memory Vault

FormPulse gets smarter every time you use it. The Memory Vault is a private, encrypted key-value store that learns from you.

📝

How it learns

After every successful AI fill, FormPulse records the pairing: the field's label and the AI's answer. The next time a field with a similar label appears on any website, the Vault match fires first — instantly, without calling the AI.

🔒

Fully Private

Vault entries are stored securely inside your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Sensitive fields (passwords, OTPs, CVV, biometrics) are automatically blocked from ever being stored.

📖
Click Manage Vault Dashboard in the popup to open a full dashboard where you can search, view, and delete individual entries. You can also export the entire vault as a JSON file for backup.

Smart filtering — what the vault rejects

Any field whose label contains keywords like password, otp, cvv, secret, or biometric is permanently blocked from being stored in the Vault. FormPulse will never learn or store your security credentials.
Fields labeled search, query, keyword, comment, or feedback are ignored. Long text blocks (over 100 characters) are also excluded to prevent cluttering the vault with essay-length answers.
The Vault doesn't require an exact label match. It uses smart text matching to match labels that are semantically similar. For example, a vault entry for Are you willing to relocate? will also match Willing to relocate or Open to relocation? on another site.

AI Engine & Tone Matching

How FormPulse uses your bio and writing samples to generate highly personalized, professional answers.

✍️

Professional Bio

The AI's knowledge base about you. Every cover letter and open-ended question is answered using specific facts from here. The more detailed this is, the more impressive the output.

🎭

Writing Samples = Your Voice

The AI reads your samples and locks onto your writing style. If you write in short, punchy sentences, so will FormPulse. If you prefer formal academic language, the AI will match it precisely.

AI behavior rules

On job boards like LinkedIn or Internshala, there may be 20+ company names in the background. FormPulse's context scanner is strictly restricted to the active modal/dialog box you opened. It will only read the company name shown inside your application popup, never from the listing list behind it.
FormPulse's AI is explicitly instructed to maintain a highly professional, polite, and formal tone at all times. It is prohibited from using casual phrases like "How are you doing?" or "Hope you're well". Every piece of writing focuses strictly on your skills, the role, and the company.
If the AI cannot find the answer in your bio (e.g., "Expected salary" when you haven't specified one), it will NOT guess or make up a number. Instead, it will leave the field blank and mark it yellow so you can fill it in correctly yourself.
Fields containing Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, OTPs, or passwords are handled exclusively by the local profile — they are stripped out before the AI prompt is built. Your national ID numbers are never included in any API request.
For yes/no questions about relocation, work shifts, background checks, or visa sponsorship, the AI defaults to the positive answer ("Yes", "Willing", "I agree") — unless your Professional Bio explicitly says otherwise. This ensures you don't accidentally appear uncooperative on applications.

This is a known limitation of the smart text matching used on dropdown (<select>) fields. Here is what happens and how to fix it:

Why it happens: Different websites list countries, states, and disciplines in slightly different formats. When FormPulse tries to match "India" against a dropdown, it compares your saved value against all available options using a similarity score. If the dropdown loads slowly (especially in JavaScript-heavy portals like Workday or SAP), FormPulse may read an incomplete or partially-rendered options list and pick the closest wrong match — like "Iceland" or "Indiana" — because the full list wasn't available yet.

Step-by-step fix:
1. Turn off AutoPilot — Open the FormPulse popup and toggle AutoPilot off. When AutoPilot is on, it fires immediately as elements appear on screen, often before the dropdown has finished loading all its options. Turning it off gives the page time to fully render.
2. Wait for the page to fully load — Ensure the entire form and all its dropdowns are visually complete before proceeding.
3. Click "Auto-Fill Page" manually — With AutoPilot off, press the button yourself. FormPulse will now scan a fully-rendered form and find the correct dropdown options.
4. If still wrong — Manually select the correct value from the dropdown, then submit the form. FormPulse will now learn not to use the Vault for that dropdown (dropdown values are no longer saved to the Vault by design, so each fill will go through the AI which adapts to the live options).
If a specific dropdown (like Country, Location, or Discipline) consistently fills with the wrong value, a stale or incorrect entry may be sitting in your Memory Vault from a previous session. Here's how to clean it up:

1. Open the FormPulse popup and click "Manage Vault Dashboard".
2. Use the search bar to look for the field name (e.g., "location", "country", "discipline").
3. Find the entry with the wrong saved value and click the Delete button next to it.
4. Close the Vault and go back to your form. FormPulse will now call the AI fresh for that field, which reads the actual live dropdown options and picks the correct one.

Pro tip: Going forward, dropdown values from <select> elements are never saved to the Vault (by design), so this should only affect entries from older sessions. New fills will always be handled by the AI, which adapts to whatever options are present on the page.
