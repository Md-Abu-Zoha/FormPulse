# SmartFill Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

SmartFill ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being completely transparent about how your data is handled.

Because SmartFill is designed to automate the filling of highly sensitive forms (like job applications and registrations), we have built the extension with a "Local-First" architecture.

### 1. Where Your Data Lives (Local Storage)
All personal profile information (such as your Name, Email, Phone Number, Date of Birth, ID document numbers, Professional Bio, and Writing Samples) and all data saved to the Memory Vault are stored **locally on your device** using your browser’s secure local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). 
**We do not own, operate, or rent any servers.** Your personal data is never transmitted to us, nor can we access it. 

### 2. How the AI Engine Uses Your Data
To generate intelligent, contextual responses (such as cover letters or complex answers), SmartFill relies on third-party AI Providers (e.g., Groq, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi). 
* When you use the "Auto-Fill Page" feature, only the context necessary to answer the question (your Professional Bio, Writing Samples, and the specific form questions) is sent directly from your browser to the AI Provider whose API Key you provided.
* **Sensitive Data Protection:** SmartFill explicitly prevents sensitive Local Profile data (such as Aadhaar, PAN, Passport numbers, and passwords) from ever being sent to the AI Provider. 
* Your API Key is securely encrypted on your device and is only used to authenticate your personal requests with your chosen provider.

### 3. Data Collection by SmartFill
We **do not** collect, track, or sell your personal information, browsing history, form submissions, or usage analytics. 

### 4. Third-Party Privacy Policies
Since SmartFill transmits specific form data to your chosen AI Provider to generate answers, that interaction is governed by the respective privacy policy of the provider you select (e.g., Google, Groq, Moonshot AI, DeepSeek). We encourage you to review their policies regarding data retention of API requests.

### 5. Your Rights and Control
You have total control over your data. 
* You can view, edit, or delete any learned answers via the "Manage Vault Dashboard".
* If you uninstall the SmartFill extension, all of your locally stored data, profile information, and Memory Vault contents are immediately and permanently deleted by your browser.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our [Support Form](https://forms.gle/kVYUG9AiRx4hNnFk6).
